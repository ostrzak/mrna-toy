# V5 proportion and clearance pass — 2026-10-30

The upper subunit is fuller and asymmetric, with a body, an offset head and a
side platform. The lower body gains a rounded, fixed palm swell below the RNA
plane. The intent is to cup that underside with one hand while the other hand
rocks the upper shell or moves the exposed RNA. This is a CAD handhold proposal;
comfort, reach, grip stability and finger clearance need a physical size mockup.

## Ribosome reference

RCSB PDB-101's [assembled ribosome](https://pdb101.rcsb.org/motm/121) and
[ribosomal subunits](https://pdb101.rcsb.org/motm/10) show substantial,
asymmetric subunits. The small subunit has a distinct head and body separated
by a cleft; it is not a small circular lid. Those broad relationships guide the
new shell. The smooth surfaces, vertical stacking and proportions remain a
stylized fidget design, not an anatomical reconstruction. “Small” and “large”
do not prescribe a width ratio, and the 30S/50S values are sedimentation units,
not dimensions.

## What changed

- Enlarged the upper shell in plan and height, with an offset head and side
  platform. Kept its axis, angular stroke, driving pin and retaining interface.
- Added a fixed underside palm swell without moving the drum, RNA plane,
  outlet, gear centers or bead path.
- Seated the separate keeper and axle in the lower shell. Widened the lid's
  openings around the moving drive leaf, holding guide and release yoke.
- Routed the cap's internal support above the return-spring space. Relieved
  its skirt around the release tab's complete lift.
- Removed yoke interpenetration at the leaf contacts and ratchet. A local
  bridge detours around the existing driving pin through its stroke and the
  full release lift. Its lift ring now passes outside the ratchet tip radius,
  with a matching lid opening, so the intermediate lift clears too. This is
  a clearance correction to the same release
  arrangement; its force transmission remains unqualified.

The gear train, 28-pocket drum, 26 beads, 6 mm spacing, three bases per bead,
22-step travel, four retained beads and reverse operation are retained. V4,
V4 Print and older comparisons are preserved.

## RNA decision remains open

The closed RNA loop is retained for this pass to preserve the existing drive.
It still surrounds the body and can obstruct a grip taken around its edge.
The new fixed underside offers a place to hold the object below that moving
plane; it does not establish that the loop is the best final interaction.

Biological mRNA passes between the subunits; it does not encircle a ribosome
as this input does. An open strand or a more localized exposed loop would need
a separate routing, retention and drive study. No such mechanism change or
choice is made here.

## Evidence and limits

Use the [saved V5 model](../cad/v5/RibosomeV5.FCStd),
[assembled view](../cad/v5/handheld.png), [grip side view](../cad/v5/grip.png)
and [fresh checks](../cad/v5/checks.json). The report records measured housing
bounds, actual bead/drum contacts, gear phases, continuous drive/protein
envelopes, native forward/reverse poses and travel stops. The expanded shell
check samples nine rocker positions at three release heights, including the
midpoint of the lift and the spring space claim.

The earlier focused suite omitted several housing/release pairs. Its pass did
not establish those clearances. The expanded check covers the clashes found
in this review. Shared rigid carrier intersections, the screw/pilot envelope
and unloaded elastic ratchet preload are identified separately in the report.
No result establishes spring behavior, click force, durability, print fits,
reliable feeding or comfortable handling.
