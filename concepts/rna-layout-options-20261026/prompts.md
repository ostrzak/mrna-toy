## Prompt A

Use case: product-mockup. Create a photorealistic industrial-design concept image of the ENTIRE wearable mRNA translation fidget toy, worn on an adult wrist. Three-quarter close view of the back of the wrist, with enough forearm and hand visible to understand scale and how the complete wearable sits on the body; soft neutral studio background and natural soft daylight, realistic skin texture, tasteful contemporary product photography. The product is elegant, compact, watch-sized, not a huge sculpture.
Shared product: a discreet matte navy wrist strap independently holds a stationary ribosome on the dorsal wrist. The ribosome is an organic, asymmetrical pair of pale-lilac biomorphic lobes, approximately 30 mm across, with a small seam between them. It is a tactile physical toy, not an electronic device; no screen, LEDs or logos. Conceal its internal mechanism.
The PROTEIN is a separate, continuous poseable chain emerging from a side port of the ribosome. Give it a slim articulated backbone with small irregular amino-acid-like side groups, with visible connecting links, warm coral and muted peach tones. It must look more like a stylized molecular chain than a string of identical round pearls. Its emerged section is manually posed into an elegant small three-dimensional curved fold with a short loose helix, close to the wrist, clearly holding its shape. Show one free terminal end; do not make a closed protein bracelet or a bulky solid blob. A few incoming protein units are neatly aligned in a small guide at the opposite protein port, with the remaining incoming length discreetly routed along the strap. This is a preassembled chain passing through the ribosome, not magical creation of beads.
The mRNA is BLUE and visibly much finer than the protein: a single narrow backbone with small projecting nucleotide tabs along ONE edge, with a few muted mint and cyan accents. No DNA double helix, no second paired strand, no railway ladder, no separate conventional sawtooth rack. Keep the RNA input and protein output visually distinct.
The main goal is to judge the aesthetics and component relationships of the whole wearable. Clean readable silhouette, coherent physically plausible connections, accurate hand anatomy, no exploded views, cutaways, mechanism diagrams, inset panels, extra products, excessive labels or measurement graphics. This is a design concept, not a fabrication drawing.
Specific concept A: The mRNA is a SHORT STRAIGHT SLIDING RIBBON, aligned with the length of the forearm, passing through the fixed ribosome. Approximately 60 mm long overall, only 3–4 mm wide, with short visible portions on either side of the ribosome. It rests in discreet low-profile guides slightly above the skin. It is a compact pull strip, not a loose dangling cord and not a strap around the wrist. Its ends are tidy and rounded; one slightly enlarged blue nucleotide serves as a tiny grip. Make the straight fine RNA ribbon clearly legible in the whole-wrist composition. The posed protein remains the main sculptural decorative feature beside the ribosome. Small unobtrusive heading at the upper left, exact text: "A · Straight RNA slider". No other text.
